Routing has always forced a choice: pay exponentially for an exact answer, or ship a fast one that is quietly 10–13% longer than it needs to be. BGMA ends that trade-off. It reproduced the exact Held–Karp optimum every time the two could be compared — in 1.4 ms. It reaches the published optimum on 9 of 14 TSPLIB instances, and where it does not, the true gap is measured: +1.05% at 783 cities and +2.10% at 1002. On every 1000-city instance whose optimum is known by construction, it returns that optimum exactly.

Why it wins: cheap search everywhere, expensive search only where it pays

Most solvers pick one gear and stay in it. BGMA runs a search party: a beam builds diverse partial tours from several starting poles, a fast 2-opt / Or-opt screen drives each to a local optimum, and an elite pool is recombined with edge crossover (ERX). Children get kicked with double-bridge moves or a short anneal, screened again, and only the handful that earn it receive the costly full-restart polish. Turn the effort dial and you buy quality with time — never the other way round.

What the gap tells you

+0.00% with OPTIMAL or PROVEN means there is nothing left to win. Anything else is exactly how much distance is still on the table.

Still skeptical? Good — that is the right instinct for a solver with no theorem attached. Drop the effort to low, roll a new seed, and watch the gap anyway.

The fine print, in full. Every number on this page comes from a run you can reproduce; nothing here is a marketing estimate. BGMA is a heuristic, and confidence should scale with evidence, not with adjectives. “Optimal” means matched on every instance we could verify, not a theorem. Up to 20 cities the target comes from Held–Karp, which needs (n−1)·2^(n−1) states and stops there. Past that, tours are scored against published TSPLIB optima (14 instances, 51–1060 cities): 9 are exact, and the n≈1000 excess is real — +2.10% on pr1002, +1.45% on u1060. A gap to the 1-tree bound is only a ceiling, and a loose one: on fl417 the bound claims 5.6% for a tour that is provably optimal, and across the set the ceiling overstates the true gap by 0.00–5.34 percentage points. The demo runs scaled-down effort budgets to stay watchable; the full --effort ladder lives in hybrid_effort.js.
